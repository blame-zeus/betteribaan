Mayor's Corner

Hon. Jane A. Casas
Municipal Mayor
Vision

A premier municipality driven by excellence in education, healthcare and world-class tourism - anchored on transparent governance, empowered communities, and sustainable development.

Mission

To transform Ibaan into a model of inclusive, resilient, and service oriented local governance by: - Establishing quality, accessible, and LGU-led higher education institutions - Advancing public healthcare systems through fully equipped facilities and partnerships - Promoting faith-based and heritage tourism as economic drivers - Strengthening agriculture, local enterprises, and infrastructure to uplift livelihoods - Upholding efficiency, transparency, and accountability through quality management systems
