Profile

Published on August 7, 2025

One of the thirty-two towns in the province of Batangas, Ibaan is a second-class municipality and one of the smallest, but among the most progressive. According to the 2020 census, there are 26 barangays, which together have 58,507 inhabitants in 14,837 households. Located 96 kilometers (60 miles) south of Manila. With five distinct communities surrounding it, its location is extremely unique. The northwest is bordered by San Jose, the northeast is Lipa City, the east is Rosario, the southeast is Taysan, and the southwest is Batangas City. It has a land area of 68.99 square kilometers (26.64 square miles) and an above-sea level elevation of 178 meters (584 feet).

Geography

Published on August 7, 2025

Ibaan is located at 13°49′03″N 121°07′59″E. It is 13 kilometres (8.1 mi) from Batangas City and 118 kilometres (73 mi) from Manila. It is bounded to the northwest by San Jose, to the northeast by Lipa City, to the east by Rosario, to the southeast by Taysan, and to the southwest by Batangas City. It has a land area of 68.99 square kilometers (26.64 sq mi) at an altitude of 124 meters (407 ft) above sea level. According to the Philippine Statistics Authority, the municipality has a land area of 68.99 square kilometers (26.64 sq mi) [7] constituting 2.21% of the 3,119.75-square-kilometer (1,204.54 sq mi) total area of Batangas.
